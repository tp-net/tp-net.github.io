Become an Organizational YTPN Representative

Partner with us to provide your members with exclusive professional development opportunities. Perfect for HR departments, graduate committees, and professional associations looking to expand their networks.

What's in it for you

Why Partner with YTPN?

Partnering with YTPN offers your organization unique opportunities to enhance member value, expand networks, and strengthen your professional community.

Member Development

Provide your members with exclusive access to professional development opportunities, workshops, and industry insights.

Expanded Networks

Connect your organization with other professional associations, industry leaders, and technical communities.

Collaborative Events

Co-host events, workshops, and networking sessions that benefit both your members and the broader technical community.

Recognition & Visibility

Gain recognition as a forward-thinking organization committed to developing the next generation of technical professionals.

How it works

The Partnership Process

Establishing a partnership with YTPN is straightforward. Here's what your organization can expect from start to finish.

1

Apply

Submit your organization's application through our form. Tell us about your organization, member base, and collaboration goals.

2

Review

Our team reviews applications and may reach out to discuss partnership opportunities and mutual benefits.

3

Partnership

Once approved, we'll establish a formal partnership agreement and provide access to our collaboration resources.

4

Collaborate

Start collaborating on events, nominating your members for opportunities, and building stronger professional networks.

Your role

Partnership Responsibilities

As a partner organization, you'll play a key role in building and maintaining our community. Here's what we expect from our organizational representatives.

Event Collaboration

Partner with YTPN to co-organize events, workshops, and conferences that benefit your members and the broader community.

Member Nominations

Nominate your qualified members for speaking opportunities, awards, and leadership positions within the network.

Network Expansion

Help expand both networks by promoting YTPN events to your members and introducing your organization to our community.

Strategic Input

Provide organizational perspective and feedback to help shape YTPN's programs and ensure they meet industry needs.

Completely Free Partnership

There's no cost to become a YTPN partner organization. We believe in building partnerships based on mutual benefit and community impact, not financial barriers.

What You Get for Free

  • Full access to our partner organization resources and collaboration tools
  • Exclusive professional development opportunities for your members
  • Co-hosting opportunities and access to industry leaders
  • Dedicated support and partnership guidance from our team
